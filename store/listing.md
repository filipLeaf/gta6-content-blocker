## Name

Content Blocker for GTA 6

## Short description (summary, max 132 chars)

Blurs or hides GTA 6 videos, posts and spoilers on YouTube, X and Reddit. Free, open source, no tracking.

## Description

Waiting for GTA 6 on PC, or just want to go in blind? This extension keeps GTA 6 out of your feed.

It blurs GTA 6 content on YouTube, X (Twitter) and Reddit before you see it: videos, Shorts, thumbnails, search results, tweets, posts and comments. New content gets blocked as you scroll.

Features:
• Strict mode: hides everything about GTA 6 (trailers, news, memes, leaks).
• Spoilers only mode: lets news through, hides endings, missions, leaks and story spoilers.
• Blur or hide completely.
• "Show anyway" button to reveal a single post.
• Turn it off on one site and keep it on the others.
• Add your own words to block, or words that should never be blocked.
• Counter of how much content was blocked.

Privacy:
No data collection, no analytics, no ads. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/filipLeaf/gta6-content-blocker
