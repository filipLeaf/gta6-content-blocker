# Privacy Policy

Content Blocker for GTA 6 does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- The extension reads the text of posts and videos on supported sites (YouTube, X, Reddit) only to decide whether to blur them. This happens locally in your browser.
- Your settings and custom words are saved with your browser's built-in extension storage. If browser sync is on, your browser may sync them between your own devices.
- The "content blocked" counter is stored locally on your device.
- The extension makes no network requests. There is no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/filipLeaf/gta6-content-blocker/issues
